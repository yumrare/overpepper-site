Table sauces are for topping; pastes are for cooking. This pineapple habanero chili paste concentrates fresh habanero peppers with pineapple into a thick, spoonable seasoning — the format Caribbean and Southeast Asian kitchens reach for when a dish needs real pepper body, not a vinegar splash. Stir a spoonful into marinades, curries, glazes, and stir-fries: the pineapple caramelizes in heat, the habanero blooms in oil, and the whole dish changes key. It is the working cook's bottle on this shelf — small, dense, and used by the spoonful until it is gone.

The heat, honestly

Concentration is the warning: pastes carry far more pepper per spoonful than pourable sauces, and this one is honestly hot — a building habanero burn with the fruit baked in. No Scoville figure is published. Start with half a teaspoon in a whole dish and adjust like you mean it.
