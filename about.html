About OverPepper

OverPepper is the hot-sauce store: twenty-four real bottles in three heat bands, and every heat figure stated honestly.

The promise

We stock it. It ships to you. OverPepper holds no warehouse of its own. When you order, we route your purchase to the source that holds your bottle — the maker or a distributor we buy from — and we stay responsible for your experience the whole way. Every heat claim on this site is a fact with its source attached: the maker’s own published figure, the number printed on the label, or an estimate openly labeled as one. If we can’t stand behind a number, the page says so instead of inventing one.

Heat, honestly

Scoville figures are slippery — the same sauce can test differently batch to batch, and some famous numbers are marketing. So each bottle’s page tells you where its figure comes from and what the burn actually does: fast or slow, front of the tongue or back of the throat, a pour or a drop. Bands — warm, hot, overpeppered — are a promise about the experience, not just a number.

Authenticity

Every bottle on these shelves is a genuine product made by the maker named on its page — the real sauce in the real bottle, never a knockoff or a relabel. Before a bottle earns a page, we verify the maker, the peppers, and the heat claim. Each page lists all three, so you can check every claim for yourself.

Questions, answered

How long will my order take to arrive?

It depends on the source. Every bottle states its expected shipping window — including longer windows for international sources — before you pay, and OverPepper launches serving US customers. Orders route to the source within one business day, and supplier tracking is relayed to you whenever it exists.

What happens if a source can’t fulfill my order?

Your refund is automatic. Your card is authorized at checkout and captured only when a source confirms your bottle is secured, so if a source falls through — sold out, or unable to ship — the authorization is voided or your payment refunded in full. We never substitute a different bottle silently.

Can I return a sauce I changed my mind about?

Food is final-sale, except when an item arrives damaged or is simply the wrong item — then we make it right. When a return is appropriate, it ships to the source supplier under its policy; OverPepper coordinates the return and issues your refund on the supplier’s confirmation.

How does OverPepper’s checkout work?

Add bottles to the cart and check out through PayPal: checking out here is a purchase from OverPepper, sourced per order from our partners. You finish on PayPal’s secure page, where your payment is authorized — not charged. OverPepper captures it only when a source confirms your bottle is secured; if sourcing falls through, the authorization is released and you were never charged. There are no affiliate links on OverPepper and no commissions behind any bottle — the price on the page is our price, full stop.
