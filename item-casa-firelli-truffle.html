Italy grows serious chilies in Calabria and serious truffles further north, and Casa Firelli puts them in one bottle. The house style is balance — chili heat as a seasoning, not a stunt — and the truffle edition adds the deep, earthy funk of black truffle to a Calabrian chili base with roasted peppers. Italians splash it on eggs, pizza, and pasta; it is one of the very few hot sauces that can sit on an Italian table without apology. Imported from Italy in the 5 oz bottle.

The heat, honestly

Medium heat, honestly worn: the Calabrian chili warmth is present and steady, and the truffle does the showing off. No Scoville figure is published. If your cooking leans Italian, this is the hot sauce that will actually get used — heat as an ingredient, not an event.
