El Yucateco has made hot sauce in Mérida, Yucatán since 1968, and this Caribbean recipe is the house blend with a twist: carrots join the habaneros, rounding the pepper's fruit into something almost tropical. It pours a deep orange, tastes brighter and a little sweeter than the classic red, and carries the habanero warmth Yucatán is famous for. In Mexico it is a table standard; in the US it is the El Yucateco bottle most people have never met — the connoisseur's pick of the line.

The heat, honestly

Commonly cited at about 5,400 SHU, which puts it at the warm band's ceiling: a real habanero glow that builds and lingers a minute, softened by the carrot's sweetness. It will not hurt you, but it will introduce itself. A few shakes season a whole plate of tacos or grilled fish.

Pour-it-on heat. Jalapeño, serrano, and habanero warmth you can use by the spoonful — flavor first, fire second.
